# Scientific & Technical Limitations

## 1. Domain & Sensor Limitations
- Standard RGB photography lacks depth and multispectral/hyperspectral chemical sensitivity; tissue oxygenation, vascular perfusion, and deep subcutaneous involvement cannot be inferred.
- Smartphone and consumer camera auto-white-balance algorithms apply non-linear transformations before raw capture is accessible.

## 2. Dataset Scale & Generalizability
- Small sample sizes in wound benchmarks risk overfitting to specific clinical camera setups, demographics, or ulcer etiologies.
- Ambiguities in ground-truth boundaries exist due to inter-observer variability among clinical dermatologists and wound nurses.
